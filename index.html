How the game runs
  • Ages 5–8 (Years 1–4): 20 minutes total on a running clock — two 10-minute halves with a 1-minute half-time.
  • Rolling substitutions — sub any time, wave from the bench.
  • Tap Sub on / Sub off — timers track everyone's court time automatically.
  • Sub waves: every 20:00 ÷ players (e.g. 2:30 with 8) the whole bench comes on for whoever has been on longest — everyone ends within ~30s of fair share.
  • Aim: every present player gets roughly equal minutes across the game.
  • Keep the on-court gender mix balanced (count shown above).
  • Timers only run while the game clock is running — pause at half-time.
